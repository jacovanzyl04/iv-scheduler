## Design Context

### Users
Staff members (nurses, receptionists, managers) and administrators at Drip4Life IV Therapy, a multi-branch IV therapy business with 4 locations. They use the scheduler daily to check shifts, manage availability, track hours, and handle inventory. Context ranges from quick mobile checks between patients to desktop admin sessions for weekly schedule generation.

### Brand Personality
Warm, approachable, professional. Drip4Life is a healthcare business that wants to feel human and inviting rather than cold and clinical. The gold-on-dark palette evokes premium warmth — like a well-lit treatment room, not a sterile hospital.

### Emotional Goals
- **Calm & organized**: Staff should feel that everything is under control. The interface should reduce anxiety about scheduling, not create it.
- **Confidence**: Users should always know where they are, what's happening, and what to do next.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark mode only. Near-black backgrounds (#080808) with gold (#e8e800) accent — the gold references IV fluid/drip branding.
- **Typography**: Bebas Neue (display/headings) + Figtree (body). Wide letter-spacing on display text for a premium feel.
- **Surfaces**: Layered dark surfaces (#111110 surface, #1a1a16 raised) with subtle borders (#1e1e18).
- **Branch identity**: Four branches color-coded (Parkview=blue, Clearwater=purple, Rosebank=pink, Clinic=orange).
- **Motion**: Staggered card entrances, smooth page transitions, hover lifts with gold glow.
- **Mobile**: Bottom tab bar with role-specific navigation, touch-friendly 36px+ targets.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth over sterility** — Healthcare doesn't have to feel cold. Gold accents, rounded corners, and smooth animations create approachability.
2. **Calm information density** — Show what matters without overwhelming. Use progressive disclosure and clear hierarchy.
3. **Instant orientation** — Every page should answer "where am I?" and "what can I do?" within 1 second.
4. **Consistency breeds confidence** — Same patterns for same actions across all pages. Predictability reduces cognitive load.
5. **Mobile-first dignity** — Mobile isn't a degraded experience. Adapt layouts thoughtfully, don't just shrink.
